    Fictional network built for demonstration and training. Names, routes, device IDs, loads, costs and timelines are invented or illustrative and do not represent any utility's actual system, tariff or interconnection rules. Screens are simplified versions of common screening practice. Power flow: balanced positive-sequence nodal solution on the same model as the ADMS simulator; light-load case uses spring midday load with DER at nameplate and the substation LTC lowered by line-drop compensation; peak case uses the summer peak hour with generation not credited, except storage under a firm peak-shaving schedule. Annual energy weights three representative days: summer (122), clear spring/fall (90) and winter/overcast (153). Fault study: bolted three-phase, synchronous machines at X″d = 0.16, inverters at 1.2 × rated current.
